# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Spanish-speaking readers looking for science fiction, fantasy, and horror; writers who want their work and author identity to be discoverable; and the editors who publish the circle's magazine and updates.

## Product Purpose

The Logia Ashogath site is the public home for its fanzine, individual works, authors, editorial updates, and future podcast. Success means a reader can find something to read, discover who made it, and follow that author beyond the site.

## Positioning

A writer-led genre-fiction hub that gives each work and author a durable, discoverable home instead of burying voices inside a single downloadable issue.

## Operating Context

The site is a Spanish-language static Jekyll site hosted on GitHub Pages at `/ashogath`. Editors maintain content in Git-backed files and publish by updating the repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Each magazine issue, story, poem, essay, author, blog entry, podcast entry, and call for submissions should have an individually linkable page where relevant.
- Works connect to their authors, issue, and genre tags; author pages collect their published works and known external links.
- The call for submissions must truthfully display an editable open or closed state. No deadline, theme, submission email, or other submission detail has been supplied.
- The Instagram account supplied by the user is `https://www.instagram.com/logiashogath/`; other author or organizational links must come from verified source material.
- A Git-backed visual editor may be configured, but hosted editing requires its own GitHub authorization and cannot be made live with repository code alone.
- The supplied magazine PDF is approximately 156 MB, above GitHub's 100 MB per-file limit. Its text can be extracted, but the original file must be optimized or hosted elsewhere before it can be committed as a downloadable asset.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Logia Ashogath, preserve the supplied black-and-white circular logo, and center the voices of writers of science fiction, fantasy, and horror. The supplied Instagram account is the confirmed social channel.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided logo: `logo_logia_ashogath.jpg`.
- User-provided magazine PDF: `revista.pdf`; its text layer includes an editorial, an essay about monstrosity, and fiction with visible author bylines.
- No confirmed submission deadline, next-issue theme, or submission email.

## Product Principles

- Make individual author voices discoverable, not just the magazine as a container.
- Keep every attribution and external author link grounded in the source.
- Make the content model simple to edit and extend.
- Make reading and navigation clear on both small and large screens.
